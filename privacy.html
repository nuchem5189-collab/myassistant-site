Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026

Scope

This policy describes how Horizon Peak Capital Inc ("the company") handles information in connection with its private assistant service. The service is used only by the company owner and is not offered to the public.

Text messages

The company phone number sends text messages only in reply to the company owner. Mobile phone numbers and text message opt-in information are not sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Reply STOP at any time to stop messages.

Google account data

With the owner's authorization, the assistant uses the Gmail API to read the owner's own messages and attachments, send email the owner asks it to send, and organize the owner's mailbox (labels, moving and archiving messages). This data is used only to carry out the owner's requests. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train general-purpose AI models. It is shared only with the service providers that operate the assistant (hosting and AI processing), solely to provide the service.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and security

Data is stored on the company's private server with access restricted to the assistant service. Access credentials are stored securely and are never included in messages.

Retention and deletion

Access to Google account data can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored copies are deleted on request.
