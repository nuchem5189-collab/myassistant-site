Privacy Policy — Horizon Peak Capital Inc

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Who this covers

Horizon Peak Capital Inc runs a private assistant service used only by the company owner. It is not available to the public.

Text messages

The company number replies only to the owner. Mobile numbers and text-message opt-in information are never sold or shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing. Reply STOP to stop messages.

Google account data

With the owner's approval, the assistant uses the Gmail API to read the owner's own messages and attachments, send email the owner asks it to send, and organize the owner's mailbox (labels, moving, archiving). This data is used only to do what the owner asks. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not used to train general AI models. It is shared only with the services that run the assistant (the hosting provider and the AI model provider that writes answers).

The assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Data is kept on the company's private server. Access to Google data can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; stored copies can be deleted on request.
